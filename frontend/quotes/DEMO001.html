The work, phase by phase

Here's what actually happens — and when.

We don't just show up and dig. Every phase has a purpose and a sequence that protects the work that came before it.

1
Foundation — Grading, Drainage & Soil Prep
Before anything goes in the ground, the ground needs to work. We'll regrade the northwest corner to redirect water away from the house, bring in engineered soil mix to give your sod the foundation it needs to thrive, and mark all utility lines. This week changes the entire fate of your lawn.
Result: No more standing water. A yard that drains correctly for the first time.
2
Hardscape — Flagstone Patio & Seating Wall
Your 400 sq ft flagstone patio comes next, while the lawn area is clear and equipment can move freely. We'll hand-set each stone for a natural, irregular pattern that looks like it was always there. The built-in seating wall creates a defined gathering space — no furniture legs sinking into grass, no rearranging for a party.
Result: A permanent outdoor living room. Ready to host before the sod even goes in.
3
Irrigation System — 6 Zones, Front & Back
Irrigation goes in before sod — always. We'll trench and run lines for a 6-zone system covering your entire property. Smart controller included, programmable by phone. You'll never drag a hose again. Your lawn waters itself at 5 AM and you wake up to green grass every morning.
Result: Set it and forget it. Your new lawn stays healthy without daily attention.
4
Sod Installation — 2,400 sq ft Premium Zoysia
Zoysia was chosen specifically for St. Louis's climate — it handles Missouri summers without burning, stays dense and weed-resistant, and feels like carpet underfoot. We'll remove the existing failing grass, lay fresh sod with tight seams, and roll it for root contact. Your lawn will look established within 30 days.
Result: A lawn that looks like it's always been there — but a hundred times better than before.
5
Planting — River Birch Trees for Privacy
Three River Birch trees along your property line, spaced to create a natural privacy screen that grows fuller every year. River Birch is Missouri native — drought-tolerant, gorgeous in every season, with peeling bark that looks intentional year-round. No maintenance beyond the occasional prune.
Result: Privacy from the neighbor's yard that increases every year without a fence.
6
Landscape Lighting
Your yard should look different at night — in the best way. We'll install 6 path lights along the walkway, 4 uplights at the base of your new trees, and 2 spotlights framing the patio. Everything is low-voltage LED, connects to a single smart controller, and transforms the after-dark feel of your entire property.
Result: The house on the block that every neighbor slows down to look at after dark.

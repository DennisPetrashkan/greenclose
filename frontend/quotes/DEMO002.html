The work

Here's everything we'll do — nothing hidden.

Every item below is included in your quote. No surprises. No extras that appear on invoice day.

🍂
Spring Cleanup — All 4 Beds
Full removal of dead material, leaves, and winter debris from all four garden beds. We haul everything away — nothing gets piled in your yard. Beds get hand-raked and edged to a clean, crisp line before mulch goes in.
Result: The bones of a beautiful yard, ready for mulch and the season.
🌿
Fresh Mulch — 6 Cubic Yards, All Beds
We'll install 3 inches of premium hardwood mulch across all four beds. Not too thick, not too thin — the right depth to hold moisture, suppress weeds, and give your beds the dark, rich look that makes the whole yard look intentional. Color: deep brown (or black if preferred).
Result: The single biggest visual upgrade you can make to a yard in one afternoon.
✂️
Shrub Trimming — East Fence Row (12 shrubs)
Your 12 arborvitae along the east fence get cut back to a consistent, clean shape. Right now they're 18–24 inches past where they should be. After trimming, they'll create the privacy screen you wanted when you planted them — instead of just looking overgrown.
Result: A clean, defined back border that actually provides privacy.
🌱
Lawn Fertilization + Pre-Emergent Weed Treatment
A slow-release spring fertilizer gives your lawn the nitrogen it needs to green up fast and stay thick all season. Pre-emergent goes down at the same time to prevent crabgrass and summer annuals before they germinate. This one application pays for itself in the weeds you won't have to pull in July.
Result: A lawn that greens up fast and stays weed-free through summer.
🔲
Full Bed Edging — All Beds + Driveway Strip
Clean mechanical edging along every bed line and the grass strip between your lawn and driveway. A properly edged yard looks like a professional landscaper maintains it year-round — this is the detail that separates "nice yard" from "wow, that yard."
Result: The sharp lines that make everything else look intentional.
